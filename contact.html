Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions.

How do I file a complaint?

Log in to your CivicAI account and open the "My Complaints" section. Select "New Complaint", choose the relevant category, describe the issue and submit the complaint with any supporting documents or photos.

How can I track my complaint?

Open "My Complaints" from your dashboard. You can view the complaint status, submitted date, assigned department and the latest available update.

What is the time taken to resolve a complaint?

Resolution time depends on the complaint category, responsible department and complexity of the issue. You can track the latest status from your complaint details page.

How do I add documents or photos?

While submitting a complaint, use the attachment option to upload supported documents or images. Make sure the files are clear and relevant to the complaint.

Is my data secure on CivicAI?

CivicAI is designed to protect account and complaint information using appropriate security controls. Avoid sharing passwords, OTPs or other sensitive account information with anyone.

Can I update my complaint after submitting it?

You can view your submitted complaint from the "My Complaints" section. If an update or additional information is required, use the available complaint update option or contact the responsible department.
