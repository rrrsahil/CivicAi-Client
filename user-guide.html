How to File a Complaint

Follow these simple steps to raise your complaint.

1

Login / Register

Login or create a new account.

2

Click on ‘File a Complaint’

Open the complaint form from your dashboard.

3

Fill Complaint Details

Add category, location and photos.

4

Submit Complaint

Review and submit your complaint.

5

Get Acknowledgement

Receive a unique complaint ID.

6

Track Complaint

Follow status in real-time.
