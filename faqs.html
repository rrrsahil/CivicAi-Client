Frequently Asked Questions

12 Questions
How do I file a complaint on CivicAI?

You can file a complaint by clicking on the "File a Complaint" button from your dashboard. Select the appropriate complaint category, provide the required details, describe the issue clearly and attach supporting photos or documents if required.

After submitting the complaint, CivicAI will generate a unique complaint ID. You can use this ID to track the status and progress of your complaint.

How can I track my complaint?

Open the "Track Complaint" section from the CivicAI navigation menu. Enter your complaint ID or select an existing complaint from your account to view its latest status.

The tracking page can display the current complaint status, assigned department and available progress updates.

What types of issues can I report?

CivicAI can be used to report civic issues such as sanitation problems, road and streetlight issues, water supply concerns, waste management, public infrastructure problems and other supported civic services.

Select the category that most closely matches your issue before submitting the complaint.

How long does it take to resolve a complaint?

Resolution time depends on the type of complaint, the responsible civic department and the complexity of the issue.

You can continue tracking the complaint through CivicAI to view the latest available progress.

Can I update my complaint after submitting?

If your complaint is still eligible for modification, additional information may be added from the complaint details page.

For complaints that have already been processed or closed, contact support for further assistance.

How do I create an account on CivicAI?

Select the "Register" option from the CivicAI website and provide the required account information. Follow the verification steps to complete your registration.

I forgot my password. What should I do?

Use the "Forgot Password" option on the login page. Enter the required account information and follow the password recovery instructions.

Is my personal information secure?

CivicAI should use appropriate security controls to protect user account and complaint information. Users should also avoid sharing passwords, OTPs or other confidential credentials.

Can I delete my account?

Account deletion availability depends on the account management policies implemented by CivicAI. Contact support if you need assistance with account deletion.

Is CivicAI mobile app available?

Mobile availability depends on the current CivicAI deployment. If a mobile application is provided, users can access it through the official supported distribution channel.

How can I contact support if I need help?

You can contact the CivicAI support team through the Contact Us page, email support or the available AI Assistant support channel.

How can I check payment information?

Payment-related information can be accessed from the relevant service or transaction section when payment functionality is available.
