Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I raise a complaint?

You can raise a complaint by clicking on "Raise a Complaint" and submitting the required details about your issue. You can also add supporting images or documents when applicable.

How does AI help in resolving complaints?

CivicAI uses AI to analyse complaint information, identify the appropriate category and help route the complaint to the concerned department.

Can I track my complaint online?

Yes. After submitting a complaint, you can use the complaint tracking feature to view its current status and available updates.

Can I upload images or documents?

Yes. Supporting images or documents can be attached to your complaint when they are relevant to the reported civic issue.

Is my data safe and secure?

CivicAI is designed to protect citizen information using appropriate security and access-control mechanisms.

What if I am not satisfied with the resolution?

You can review the complaint status and available resolution information. If further action is required, use the available support or feedback options to communicate your concern.
