1 Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you provide to us when registering, filing a complaint, or contacting us.

This may include your name, email address, phone number, location, and any other details you submit.

2 How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to provide, operate, and improve CivicAI services, process your complaints, send updates and notifications, and communicate with you.

3 Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

4 Information Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share your data with government authorities or third-party service providers only when necessary to resolve your complaint or as required by law.

5 Your Rights & Choices

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal information. You may also opt out of non-essential communications at any time.

6 Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience, analyze usage, and personalize content.

You can control cookie settings through your browser.

7 Children's Privacy

CivicAI is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If we learn that we have collected such data, we will delete it promptly.

8 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with the updated effective date. Please review this policy periodically.
